FAQ

Fair questions.

Another events app?

No. Weekline doesn't sell tickets or list everything. It turns what's on into one week you can send. Checkout stays with the venue.

Are weeks padded with fake events?

No. Each row says Live Parks, Sample or Editorial. If a night has nothing that fits, it says open.

Why are some nights empty?

On purpose. Only NYC Parks is live today, so thin weeks stay thin instead of being filled with filler.

Do I need to sign up to look?

The demo is an early build you can try now. The invite form is for the early group; it saves nothing yet.

What does it cost?

Planning is free. Free events and addresses will never sit behind a paywall.

Is it AI?

Ranking is plain code: your crew, radius and budget. Rows are real or labeled. No chatbot.
