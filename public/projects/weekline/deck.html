Go-to-market

Show the Sheet in 3 seconds. Soft invite before paid.

Instagram (Meta)

NYC 25–40, Brooklyn + Manhattan zips. 3 angles: "idk you pick", Free Week Brooklyn, Seeking fitness. 2-week test, $240.

Keep an angle at ≥10% invite requests or ≤$3 each. targets

Soft invite + communities

1–2 Brooklyn creators or run clubs, r/nyc and r/AskNYC posts with a real, labeled week.

The shared Sheet is the growth loop.

First 1,000 planners: 50 soft invites → shared Sheets → weekly "Free Week Brooklyn" post → one newcomer campus pilot. Never bid "dating app" or "AI trip planner".

Budget and thresholds are the planned test (assumptions); nothing has run yet. Nothing posts or sends without the founder pressing go.10 / 12
