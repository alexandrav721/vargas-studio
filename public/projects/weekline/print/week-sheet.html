The Sunday ritual

  1. Pick a crew, a zone, a budget. One crew per week.
  2. Fill the week. In the Weekline app or by hand on the Week sheet.
  3. Tick where each row came from. Live, Sample or Editorial.
  4. Leave open nights open. Thin weeks are fine.
  5. Send it to your people. Photo of the sheet or Share in the app.
  6. Put one Seeking row on the calendar. That's the proof.
