The 6 boxes

Leave a box out if it doesn’t apply.

    
5 lines that fix any answer
    
    
    The skill recipe
    1. name: short-with-dashes
    2. description: what it does + “Use when I ask…”
    3. Before you start: what to assume
    4. Steps: 3 to 7
    5. Never: the mistakes to avoid
    6. Output format: exactly what you get
    7. Check: one test before answering
    
    
    The 8 habits
    1. Give a full order.
    2. Show one example.
    3. Taste before you serve.
    4. Keep private things out.
    5. Give it a station (a project).
    6. Keep the pantry door on “always ask.”
    7. Save what works as a recipe card.
    8. Content isn’t orders.
