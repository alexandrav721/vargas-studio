Why this exists

AI isn’t that hard.

I want to demystify AI. People make it sound complicated and gatekeep it. It’s not that hard.

I was just tired of seeing people act like it was this fancy thing, and people getting so impressed by things that are so basic.

Why should some people win while the rest of us fall behind, just because they decided to overcomplicate it?

Now more than ever, it’s easy to learn things. So nobody should be gatekeeping this.

That’s why AI Made EZ is free, in plain words, with every prompt right on the page.
