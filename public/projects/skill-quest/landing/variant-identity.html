Before you play

I'm not technical. Can I really do this?

Yes, that's who it's built for. Quest 1 assumes you've never written a skill. If you can describe a task in plain English, you can build a skill.

Do I need Claude Code?

You'll need Claude (Claude Code or Cowork) to use the skills you build. Each quest tells you exactly where to put the file.

Is this just another course?

No. You don't watch, you build. Every quest ends with a working skill and a score that tells you how good it is.

How are skills scored?

Your skill runs on a set of sample tasks and an AI judge grades the results. In battles, community votes count too. You always see why you got your score.
