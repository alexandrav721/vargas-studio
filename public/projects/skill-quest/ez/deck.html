How it works

We ask the questions. You pick.

  1. One big question at a time. Tap, type or speak.

  2. "What I'll ask you": Quick, Good or Perfect.

  3. Watch it build live. Pick 1 of 3 versions.

  4. Change one thing. Print, save or copy.
