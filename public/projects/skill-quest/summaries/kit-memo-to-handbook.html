AI Made EZ · kit 6 of 6

Voice memo to handbook

A clear checklist and how-to guide for a task you explain out loud, so someone else can do it. · about 20–30 min · Free

What you need

  • Do the task once while talking through it out loud, or just explain it from memory.
  • Take out names and anything private.

Pick a tool

  • Your phone (Free): Record a voice memo. Many phones show a transcript; or speak into the chat app’s microphone.
  • Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini (Free): Paste the transcript.

Keep the <labels>. Type over the [brackets].

01Record and get the words

Record yourself explaining the task. Copy the transcript, messy is fine.

Check: You have the words as text.

02Turn it into a handbook

Paste into a new chat:

ORDER #002copy · paste · send
<goal>Turn my rambling explanation into a checklist and a short how-to guide a new person can follow.</goal>
<who_its_for>[who will use it: a new hire, a helper, future me]</who_its_for>
<context>My transcript:
[paste]</context>
<format>1) A numbered checklist with times. 2) A short guide: why each step matters. 3) Questions I didn’t answer.</format>
<rules>Keep every number, time and warning I said. Don’t add steps I didn’t say; put guesses under Questions.</rules>

Check: Every time and number from your memo is in the checklist.

03Answer the questions

Reply with answers to its questions, then ask for the final version to print.

Check: A new person could do it without calling you.

Stuck?

  • It added steps I never said Say “Remove anything not in my transcript; list it as a question instead.”
  • The transcript is a mess That’s fine. AI is good at messy. Just fix names it misheard.
