Your first website

A one-page website with a real link you can text to a friend. · about 45–60 min · Free

What you need

  • Who the site is for, and the one thing they should do (order, book, call).
  • Your real words: what you offer, prices, hours, address.
  • 2 or 3 real photos. Finding photos is where an hour becomes three.
  • A laptop is easier than a phone for this one.

Pick a tool

  • Bolt (bolt.new) (Free): Start here. Sign up free, paste the sheet into the chat box. Publish gives a free public link. A free allowance that refills daily.
  • Google AI Studio (Free): Personal Google accounts can publish up to 2 apps free (not work or school accounts).
  • Lovable (Free): 5 build credits a day. Free sites are invite-only: up to 10 people by email.
  • Claude or ChatGPT (Paid for a link): Both can build it on Free. A public link needs Claude Pro, or ChatGPT Plus (build it as a Site, Deploy, Share).

Keep the <labels>. Type over the [brackets].

01Fill in the site sheet

Copy it, fill every [bracket], keep it open.

ORDER #001copy · paste · send
<goal>Build a one-page website for [business]. Visitors should [one action: order / book / call].</goal>
<who_its_for>[who visits, and what they want to know fast]</who_its_for>
<context>Sections, in this order: 1) [one-line headline] 2) [what I offer, with prices] 3) [about me, 2 sentences] 4) [hours and address] 5) [contact button]. My real words: [paste]. Photos: leave clearly marked photo boxes, I’ll add mine.</context>
<example>The feel I want: [for example: warm, lots of space, big photos, like a neighborhood shop]</example>
<format>One page. Designed for a phone first. One big button for [the action]. Easy-to-read text.</format>
<rules>Use only the facts I gave. No made-up reviews, prices or awards. No stock photos of people. Ask me before adding pages.</rules>

Check: No empty [brackets] left.

02Paste it into Bolt

Go to bolt.new, sign up free, and paste the whole sheet into the chat box. (Any tool from the list works.)

Check: You see a page with your headline and your prices.

03Fix the words

Read it as a stranger. Then paste:

ORDER #003copy · paste · send
Change only the words. Make the headline say what I sell in under 8 words. Remove anything I didn’t tell you.

Check: Every sentence is true about your business.

04Check it on a phone

Paste:

ORDER #004copy · paste · send
Check the page at phone size. Make the button big and easy to tap, and make sure nothing runs off the side.

Check: Use your tool’s preview at phone size, or open it on your phone.

05Add your photos and publish

Attach a photo in the chat and say “Put this in photo box 1.” Repeat. Then click Publish.

Check: Open the link on your phone. Text it to one person.

Stuck?

  • It broke after a change Stop clicking Fix (each try uses up your free allowance). Go back to the last version that worked, describe what you see in plain words, and add “Explain what went wrong first. Don’t change anything yet.”
  • I ran out of free credits They come back daily, up to a monthly cap. Come back tomorrow, or switch to Google AI Studio.
  • I want my own .com Custom domains are on paid plans. You don’t need one for version 1.
  • It made up reviews or prices Say “Delete anything I didn’t give you.” This is why the rules box exists.
