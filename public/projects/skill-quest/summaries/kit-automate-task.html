AI Made EZ · kit 4 of 6

Automate one boring task

One small automation running on its own, with AI helping you plan and write it. · about 45–60 min · Free

What you need

  • A list of what you do every week.
  • Pick one task: 10+ minutes, same steps every time, harmless if it goes wrong.
  • No Google Form yet? forms.google.com → Blank. Add the questions (name, what they need, date). Then Responses → Link to Sheets, and Send to get the link.

Pick a tool

  • Zapier Free (Free): Use this one. A Zap is “when this happens” + “do this”. Copilot is Zapier’s chat box: type what you want and it builds the Zap. 100 tasks (runs) a month, checks every 15 minutes.
  • Zapier’s 14-day trial (Free 14 days): Starts on its own when you sign up, no card. Allows extra steps, which stop when it ends.
  • Claude Cowork (Paid): Does it from plain words on a schedule. Start with tasks that only read and draft.

Keep the <labels>. Type over the [brackets].

01Let AI pick the task and plan it

Paste this into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini (free is fine).

ORDER #001copy · paste · send
<goal>Help me automate one boring task, starting small.</goal>
<context>My weekly tasks: [list]. My apps: [for example Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Forms]. I’m on Zapier Free: one trigger and one action, 100 tasks a month.</context>
<format>1) The best task to start with, and why. 2) “When this happens” → “do this”, in plain words. 3) The exact request to paste into Zapier Copilot. 4) How to test it.</format>
<rules>It may only read or write drafts (like a Gmail draft I check). Never send, pay or delete on its own. Skip anything my apps already do by themselves (Google Forms already saves answers to a sheet). If it needs more than 2 steps, give me the 2-step version.</rules>

Check: You get one “when this happens” and one “do this”, in plain words.

02Build it with Copilot

Sign in to Zapier, paste the Copilot request from step 1, and follow its steps to connect your apps.

Check: Zapier shows your trigger and your action.

03Test with one real example

Send one real test (fill in your form once, or send yourself the email).

Check: Zapier’s test shows your data. Then click Publish.

04Watch the first run

Do the real thing once and wait. Free plans check every 15 minutes.

Check: The draft, row or note appears on its own. Screenshot it: that’s your proof.

Stuck?

  • Connecting my account fails Allow pop-ups for zapier.com, then try again.
  • The test finds no data Send one real example first (fill in the form once), then test again.
  • It says I need more steps Use the 2-step version from step 1. (A new account’s 14-day trial allows more, until it ends.)
  • Nothing happened Wait 15 minutes. Then open Zap history (Zapier’s list of every run) and look for errors.
