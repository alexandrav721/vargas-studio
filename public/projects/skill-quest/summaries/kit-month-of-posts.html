AI Made EZ · kit 5 of 6

A month of posts

12 posts in your voice and a simple calendar, ready to schedule. · about 30–45 min · Free

What you need

  • Your voice profile from kit 1. No profile? Paste 2 captions you wrote instead.
  • What’s happening this month: dates, new things, prices.
  • 1 or 2 past posts that did well.

Pick a tool

  • Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini (Free): Any of them. The prompt is the same.

Keep the <labels>. Type over the [brackets].

01Fill in the month sheet

Paste into a new chat.

ORDER #001copy · paste · send
<goal>Write 12 posts for [platform] for [month]: 3 a week.</goal>
<who_its_for>[your followers, and why they follow you]</who_its_for>
<context>My voice profile: [paste]. This month: [dates, new things, prices, events]. Two posts that did well: [paste].</context>
<format>A table: date, post, photo idea. Each post under [40] words.</format>
<rules>Use only my facts and dates. No hashtag walls. No words I’d never say.</rules>

Check: Every date and price matches your list.

02Cut the weak ones

Paste:

ORDER #002copy · paste · send
Mark the 3 weakest posts and rewrite them more like the two that did well.

Check: You’d post all 12.

03Put them in a calendar

Copy the table into a sheet or your scheduler.

Check: 12 rows, 12 dates.

Stuck?

  • They all sound the same Say “Mix it up: 4 news, 4 behind the scenes, 4 questions to followers.”
  • It invented an event Say “Remove anything not on my list.”
