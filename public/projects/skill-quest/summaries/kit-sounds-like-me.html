AI Made EZ · kit 1 of 6

Sounds like me

A saved voice profile, so AI writes closer to how you talk, plus 3 reply templates. · about 20–30 min · Free

What you need

  • 3 to 5 messages you actually wrote: texts, emails or posts.

Pick a tool

  • Claude (Free): Make a Project (Free includes up to 5), then Set project instructions, paste, Save.
  • ChatGPT (Free): Save it in Settings → Personalization → Custom instructions.
  • Gemini (Free): Customize → Skills → Create → Create manually. (Gems closed to new ones on 17 Nov 2026.)

Keep the <labels>. Type over the [brackets].

01Paste your messages

Copy this into a new chat. Swap in your own messages.

ORDER #001copy · paste · send
<goal>Write a voice profile of how I write, so you can write like me later.</goal>
<context>My real messages:
[paste 3 to 5 messages you wrote]</context>
<format>Under 150 words. Sections: Tone, Words I use, Length, How I start, How I end, Never.</format>
<rules>Only describe what you can see in my messages. Quote my real phrases. Don’t make me sound fancier than I am.</rules>

Check: It quotes your real words. If it sounds like a stranger, say “Closer to my messages, less polished.”

02Save it

Copy the profile into the place your app keeps instructions (see the tool list above).

Check: Open a new chat (in Claude, inside your Project) and ask “What do you know about how I write?”

03Make 3 reply templates

In a new chat with your profile saved, list the 3 messages you answer most and what you usually say back.

ORDER #003copy · paste · send
<goal>Write reply templates for the 3 messages I answer most.</goal>
<context>Use my voice profile. The 3 messages, and what I usually say back: [1] … [2] … [3] …</context>
<format>For each: the template with [brackets] for the parts that change. Under 60 words each.</format>
<rules>Sound like my profile. No apologies unless I’d apologize.</rules>

Check: Read one out loud. If you wouldn’t send it, say what’s off.

Stuck?

  • It sounds too formal Paste 2 more casual messages and say “Match these.”
  • It forgets my voice in new chats It only remembers if you saved it (step 2).
  • It copies my messages word for word Say “Use my style, not my sentences.”
