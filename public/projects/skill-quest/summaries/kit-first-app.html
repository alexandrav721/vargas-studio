AI Made EZ · kit 3 of 6

Your first app

A small working tool, like a price calculator, that saves what you enter and has a link. · about 45–60 min · Free

What you need

  • Shrink the idea: one person, one screen, one thing saved.
  • Write what “done” looks like in one sentence.

Pick a tool

  • Google AI Studio (Free): Start here: aistudio.google.com, then Build. Your app stays private until you share it.
  • Bolt (bolt.new) (Free): Free public link. Each “Attempt fix” uses some of your daily allowance.
  • Lovable (Free): Free apps are invite-only: up to 10 people by email.
  • Claude (Paid to save and share): Builds small tools on Free. Saving between visits and a public link need Pro.

Keep the <labels>. Type over the [brackets].

01Fill in the app sheet

Small wins. Logins and payments are a later kit.

ORDER #001copy · paste · send
<goal>Build a small app that [does one job] for [who].</goal>
<context>On the screen: [the inputs, with choices]. It shows: [the result, with your real rules or prices]. It saves: [what to keep, for example a list of past quotes].</context>
<format>One screen, phone first. Big inputs. Show the result as soon as something changes.</format>
<rules>Use exactly my rules and prices. No logins, no payments. Saved items must still be there after I refresh the page. They can stay on this one device.</rules>

Check: Your real rules and prices are in the sheet.

02Build the screen

Paste the sheet as your first message.

Check: Change an input. Does the result change?

03Test the saving

Add 3 items, then refresh the page.

Check: Still there? If not, paste: “My items disappear when I refresh. Make them save.”

04Try to break it

Paste:

ORDER #004copy · paste · send
Test this app like a picky customer: empty boxes, huge numbers, the wrong order of steps. Fix what breaks, and tell me what you changed.

Check: Nothing shows a blank, $0 for a real order, or an error.

05Share it

Click Share or Publish, then send the link to a friend.

Check: Your friend opens it and gets a price. (Saved items stay on each person’s own device.)

Stuck?

  • My data disappears on refresh Saving isn’t switched on. Ask: “Save the items in this browser so they survive a refresh.”
  • It works for me but not my friend The app is still private. Click Share or Publish. On Lovable Free, invite them by email.
  • It keeps getting bigger Say “Undo the last change. Keep it to one screen.”
  • The math is wrong Give it a worked example: “6-inch, 2 layers, delivery should be $43.”
