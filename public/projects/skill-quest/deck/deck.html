Business model

Three payers: sponsors first, then players, then companies.

1 · Sponsors

$500+

AI tool companies host a weekly battle on their tool. First revenue, no paywall needed.

2 · Pro players

$8/mo

$72/yr. Free tier capped at 5 quests + 3 cards.

3 · Teams

$20/seat

Company AI-upskilling battles. Similar products: ~$25–27.50/seat.

Pro gross margin ≈ 68% (ASSUMPTION: ~$0.10 AI cost per card test). Break-even ≈ 830 Pro, or 3 sponsored battles a month. [TODO measure AI cost in Battle #0]

Go-to-market

The cards are the marketing.

1 · Card content

"We scored the 10 most popular skills": free, shareable, built for Reddit and X.

2 · Community ads

Low-cost Reddit ads in r/ClaudeAI and r/ClaudeCode to test messaging.

3 · Sponsored battles

AI tool companies and creators host battles and bring their own audiences.

First 1,000 players: waitlist from card posts → invite in waves of 100 → every card shared links back to the game.
