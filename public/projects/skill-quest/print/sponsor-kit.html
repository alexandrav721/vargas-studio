AI MADE EZ · SPONSOR KIT

Host a weekly skill battle on your tool

AI Made EZ is a game that teaches beginners to build Claude skills. Each week, one battle: everyone's skill runs the same hidden tasks, gets scored, and lands on a leaderboard. You pick the theme. Builders spend the week learning your tool.

YOU GET

  • A battle named for your tool: “Best skill built with ___”
  • Your logo and prize on the battle page and every result card
  • A results recap for your team: top skills, where builders got stuck, what they asked for
  • Permission to share the winning skills (builders opt in)

WHAT STAYS FAIR

  • Same hidden tasks for every entry
  • AI judge with a published rubric, plus community votes
  • Your tool gets no special treatment from the judge
  • Every builder sees why they got their score
