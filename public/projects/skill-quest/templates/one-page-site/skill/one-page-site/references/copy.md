# Copy rules for a personal site

The page should sound like this one person talking, not like a brochure about them.

## Specific beats pretty
- Replace every adjective with a fact from their answers: a number, a name, a place, a year, a material, a client. "Stoneware fired to cone 10 in a garage in West Asheville" beats "handcrafted pieces made with love".
- Test each line: could another person in the same field paste it onto their site unchanged? Then rewrite it with their details, or cut it.
- Use the numbers they gave exactly. Never round them up, never invent new ones. If a caption has no fact yet, write `[year]` or `[how many]` and list it in your reply.

## One point of view
- Write in first person ("I make", "I draw") in the About and contact. Captions can be plain labels.
- Include one opinion or one limit, taken from what they said or clearly implied: what they don't do, who it's not for, how they like to work. If they gave none, use a real constraint they mentioned (a waitlist, one commission a month, only in person).

## Plain and short
- Sentences under 18 words. Use "is" and "has", not "serves as" or "boasts".
- Headings are short sentence-case statements or plain labels ("Work", "About", "Say hello" is fine; "Get in touch" is fine). No questions as headings, no Title Case.
- Total page copy under 300 words. The work should do the talking.
- At most one em dash on the whole page, none in headings. Use commas, colons or full stops.
- No exclamation marks except one, if the person writes that way.
- Lists: avoid lists of exactly three adjectives or skills. The three works are fine because they are real things.

## Words that make it read as AI (never use)
passionate, journey, crafting (as a verb for anything not literally crafted), elevate, unlock, empower, seamless, curated, vibrant, delve, tapestry, testament, leverage, meaningful experiences, bring your vision to life, where creativity meets, in today's world, whether you're, not just X but Y, it's not about X it's about Y, let's create something amazing together, I'm a creative.

## Captions
Format each work caption as: title, then one line of facts in this order where known: what it is, for whom or where, when, the number. Example:
"Tide bowls. Series of 40, sold out in two hours at the Asheville Fall Pottery Market, 2025."

## Contact line
Say what to write about and any real timing: "Write to me about commissions and wholesale. Next opening is March." Never "Let's connect!" or "Don't hesitate to reach out."
