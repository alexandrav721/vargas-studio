About

I make stoneware in my garage studio in West Asheville. I've been working with clay for about six years, and full time since 2023.

Everything is fired to cone 10, and I mix my own glazes. My favorite is a pale green celadon, the color you see on this page.

A few things I'm proud of

The Tide bowls

A series of 40 bowls with a dark iron rim. They sold out in about 2 hours at the Asheville Fall Pottery Market in 2025.

120

Plates for Little Ostra

In 2024 I made 120 dinner plates for Little Ostra, the seafood restaurant on Haywood Rd. It took five months.

150

Students taught

I teach a beginner wheel class at Odd Clay co-op every Tuesday night, with 8 people max. About 150 people so far.
