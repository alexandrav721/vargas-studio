# Water Cycle Quiz (Year 8 Science)

Try it without looking at your notes first. Answers are at the bottom.

## Part A: Multiple choice

**1.** What powers the water cycle?
A) The wind
B) The sun
C) The moon
D) Ocean currents

**2.** What is water vapour?
A) Water as a liquid
B) Water as a solid
C) Water as a gas
D) Salty water

**3.** What is the name for plants losing water vapour through tiny holes in their leaves?
A) Condensation
B) Infiltration
C) Transpiration
D) Collection

**4.** Why does water vapour turn into droplets as it rises?
A) The air gets warmer
B) The air gets colder
C) The sun gets stronger
D) The wind pushes it down

**5.** Which of these is NOT a form of precipitation?
A) Sleet
B) Hail
C) Snow
D) Water vapour

**6.** What do we call water that soaks into the ground and stays there for a long time?
A) Surface runoff
B) Groundwater
C) A reservoir
D) Cloud water

**7.** About what percentage of Earth's water is fresh water?
A) 3%
B) 25%
C) 50%
D) 97%

**8.** Where is most of Earth's fresh water found?
A) Rivers
B) Lakes
C) Frozen in ice caps and glaciers
D) In clouds

## Part B: True or false

**9.** Warmer temperatures mean more evaporation.

**10.** The total amount of water on Earth keeps getting smaller as it is used up.

**11.** Surface runoff is water that runs over the ground into rivers.

**12.** Condensation is when liquid water turns into a gas.

## Part C: Fill in the blank

**13.** Evaporation + transpiration together = ______________.

**14.** Lots of tiny droplets together make ______________.

**15.** A store of water such as a lake or an ice cap is called a ______________.

## Part D: Short answer

**16.** Put these in the correct order: precipitation, evaporation, condensation, collection.

**17.** Explain what happens to water in the water cycle when it evaporates from the sea. Use the words *sun*, *water vapour* and *state change*.

**18.** Cutting down lots of trees may make the air above drier. Explain why, using the word *transpiration*.

**19.** Describe two different things that can happen to water after it falls as rain.

---

## Answers

1. B
2. C
3. C
4. B
5. D
6. B
7. A
8. C
9. True
10. False. The total amount stays about the same. It just changes state and place.
11. True
12. False. That is evaporation. Condensation is a gas (vapour) turning into liquid droplets.
13. Evapotranspiration
14. Clouds
15. Reservoir
16. Evaporation, condensation, precipitation, collection
17. The sun heats the sea water, causing a state change from liquid to water vapour (a gas), which then rises.
18. Trees release water vapour through transpiration. With fewer trees there is less transpiration, so less water vapour goes into the air and it can get drier.
19. Any two: it collects in oceans, lakes and rivers; it runs over the ground as surface runoff into rivers; it soaks into the ground (infiltration) and becomes groundwater.
