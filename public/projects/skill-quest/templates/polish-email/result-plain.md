Here's a polished version. It stays friendly but is clear and firm about payment.

**Subject: Payment due: Brightwell Bakery invoice ($1,850)**

Hi Marcus,

I'm following up on the invoice for the Brightwell Bakery logo and menu design. I sent it on September 30, and payment was due October 30. It's now almost four weeks overdue. I've attached it again for convenience.

- **Amount due:** $1,850
- **Original due date:** October 30

Could you please arrange payment by the end of this week? I'll also need the invoice settled before I can start on the window signs.

Separately, the menu changes you requested last week (the extra gluten-free page and the updated prices) were outside the original quote. I should have said so at the time. I'll send a separate invoice of about $200 for that work. Let me know if you'd like to talk it over first.

I've enjoyed working on this project and look forward to finishing the window signs. Thanks for your help getting this sorted.

Best,
Talia

---

**What I changed and why**

- **Cut the apologies.** "Sorry to keep bugging you" and "sorry again" made the request sound like a favor. You're asking for payment you're owed, and you can do that politely without apologizing.
- **Removed the frustration.** "Chase this 3 times" and "I delivered everything on time??" are understandable, but they'd put Marcus on the defensive. The facts (dates, amount, how overdue) make the point without them.
- **Gave a clear deadline.** "This week" is now a specific request with a reason, which is the window signs.
- **Separated the extra $200.** Mixed in with the overdue invoice, it could give Marcus an excuse to delay the whole payment. It's now its own short paragraph, and the original invoice stays the priority.
- **Added a subject line** so the invoice is easy to find.

**Two things to consider**

1. If the $200 is a firm number, say "$200" rather than "about $200". Vague amounts invite haggling. Better still, send the extra work as its own invoice with a short breakdown.
2. If you've already chased this several times, you could add a line such as "Please let me know if there's anything holding up payment." It gives him an easy way to explain a delay. It also leaves a written record if you have to escalate, for example with a late fee, if your contract allows one.

**Shorter, firmer option** (if the first version feels too soft):

> Hi Marcus, invoice #[number] for $1,850 (Brightwell Bakery logo and menu design) was due October 30 and is now almost four weeks overdue. It's attached again. Please pay by Friday.
