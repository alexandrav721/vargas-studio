# Copy rules for a small business page

Write like the owner talking to a neighbor at the counter: plain, specific, a little proud.

## Specific beats pretty
- Every claim becomes a fact from their answers: a price, a time, a street, a number of dogs, a size, a name. "Sourdough out of the oven at 7:30, usually gone by 11" beats "freshly baked artisan bread daily".
- Test each line: could a competitor two streets over paste it unchanged? Then rewrite it with their details, or cut it.
- Use their numbers exactly. Never invent a number, a review, a rating or a years-in-business figure. Missing facts become `[slot]`s you list in the reply.
- Prices: write "$24" not "$24.00", "from $24" when it varies, add-ons as "+$8 second dog".

## One voice
- Pick "I" (a solo owner) or "we" (a team) from how they wrote, and keep it everywhere.
- Include one point of view or one limit they gave or clearly implied: "Max 4 dogs, always", "No pre-sliced bread", "Ages 8 to 14 only". Limits build trust.

## Customer words, not business words
- Name things the way a customer would ask for them: "a 30-minute walk", not "an enrichment solution"; "birthday cakes", not "celebration offerings".
- The call to action is a verb plus the real thing ("Text Dani to book", "Order a cake"). Never "Get started", "Learn more", "Contact us", "Submit".

## Plain and short
- Sentences under 16 words. "Is" and "has", not "serves as" or "boasts".
- Headings are short sentence-case statements or plain labels ("Prices", "How to book", "Hours"). No questions as headings except inside the FAQ. No Title Case.
- Under 400 words total. At most one em dash on the page, none in headings. One exclamation mark at most.
- Avoid three-adjective lists ("fresh, local and delicious").

## Words that make it read as AI (never use)
elevate, unlock, seamless, curated, vibrant, passion/passionate, journey, experience (as a noun for a service), tailored, bespoke (unless they said it), solutions, top-notch, premier, nestled, in the heart of, look no further, whether you're, we pride ourselves, quality meets, your one-stop shop, peace of mind, not just X but Y, delve, leverage, empower.

## Placeholder text (when proof is missing)
Use exactly this kind of honest note, styled as an empty spot, never as a fake review:
"Your first review goes here. Ask a regular for two sentences and their first name."
"Photo of your sourdough loaf goes here."
