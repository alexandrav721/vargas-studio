Why Two Blocks

  • Never more than 4 dogs in a group, always.
  • Insured and pet CPR certified.
  • GPS map and photo after every walk.
  • One person. Dani Reyes walks your dog herself, and she already walks about 22 regular dogs in the neighborhood.

Good to know

  • Group walks are for dogs over 1 year old. Puppies get drop-in visits.
  • In pouring rain or a thunderstorm, we do a shorter walk, and Dani texts you first.
