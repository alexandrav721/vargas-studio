# Weekly Meal Plan: Family of 4, Peanut-Free, About $120

Mild flavors, 30 minutes or less on weeknights, Mexican and pasta favorites.

**Peanut safety:** Check labels on everything, especially tortillas, sauces, granola bars, and anything "made in a facility with peanuts". Skip peanut oil. Use olive or vegetable oil.

## The Week

| Day | Dinner | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Chicken quesadillas with corn and black beans, side of salsa and sliced cucumber | 25 min |
| Tue | Spaghetti with meat sauce and a green salad | 30 min |
| Wed | Beef taco night (mild seasoning), with lettuce, cheese, tomatoes | 25 min |
| Thu | Creamy chicken and broccoli penne | 30 min |
| Fri | Sheet-pan chicken fajitas (peppers and onions, mild seasoning) with rice | 30 min |
| Sat | Homemade cheese and veggie pizza night, with the kids helping | 40 min |
| Sun | Slow cooker chicken tortilla soup, with toppings and leftover rice | 15 min prep |

## Breakfasts (rotate)
- Scrambled eggs and toast with fruit
- Oatmeal with banana and cinnamon
- Yogurt with berries (use a peanut-free granola)

## Lunches
- Turkey and cheese wraps with carrot sticks and apple slices
- Leftover quesadilla, pasta, or soup, reheated

## Snacks
- Fruit, string cheese, popcorn, tortilla chips with mild salsa, cucumbers and hummus (check the label)

## Grocery List (estimated about $115 to $120)

**Produce (about $26)**
- Bell peppers x4, onions x3, garlic x1 head
- Broccoli x2 crowns, cucumbers x3, carrots 2 lb
- Lettuce x2, tomatoes x5, limes x2
- Bananas x1 bunch, apples x6, berries x1 pack (frozen is cheaper)
- Avocado x2 (optional)

**Meat (about $34)**
- Chicken breasts or thighs, about 4 lb
- Ground beef, 2 lb (use half for tacos, half for the spaghetti sauce)
- Deli turkey, 1 lb

**Dairy and eggs (about $18)**
- Shredded Mexican cheese 2 bags, mozzarella 1 bag, string cheese
- Eggs x1 dozen, milk x1 gallon, heavy cream or cream cheese (for the penne)
- Yogurt, large tub

**Pantry (about $32)**
- Spaghetti 1 lb, penne 1 lb, rice 2 lb
- Flour tortillas x2 packs, taco shells x1 box
- Black beans x2 cans, corn x2 cans (or frozen)
- Pasta sauce x1 jar, canned diced tomatoes x2, chicken broth x2 cartons
- Mild salsa, pizza dough (or flour and yeast), pizza sauce
- Oatmeal, bread, popcorn kernels, tortilla chips
- Spices: chili powder, cumin, paprika, garlic powder, Italian seasoning (most are likely already in your cupboard)

**Tip:** Buy store-brand items and check your pantry first. That can bring the total under $110.

## Time-Savers for Weeknights
- **Sunday:** Chop peppers, onions, and broccoli. Cook the rice for the week. Brown the beef for Tuesday and Wednesday in one batch.
- **Slow cooker or Instant Pot:** Sunday soup lets you make extra chicken for Monday's quesadillas.
- **Shred the cheese yourself** when you have time. It's cheaper than bagged.
- Let the 11-year-old run the taco bar. Let the 7-year-old build pizzas.

## Mild-Spice Notes
- Use chili powder and cumin for flavor, not heat. Skip chipotle, jalapeno, and cayenne.
- Put hot sauce and jalapenos on the table for the adults only.

## Swaps
- **Cheaper:** Use ground turkey or beans for tacos, or skip the avocado.
- **More veggies:** Add spinach to the meat sauce and zucchini to the fajitas.
- **Leftovers:** Pasta and soup both keep well for lunches.
