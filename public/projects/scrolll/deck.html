How it works

A view only counts when half the post is on screen for one full second.

1 · you

Set your floor

What one look at you costs. Sample range 0.5¢ to 3¢.

2 · a brand

A brand bids

Every sponsored post is labeled and shows its offer.

3 · seen = counted

Half on screen, 1 second

Video needs 2 seconds. Scroll away and the timer resets.

4 · done

Receipt, then ads stop

At your hourly cap the ads end. The feed stays open.

Competition

Others pay you in points. Nobody lets you set the price.

Brave is closest: opt-in ads, an hourly cap, a big user share. It lives in a browser. Scrolll is a feed with a price floor and a receipt for every ad.
