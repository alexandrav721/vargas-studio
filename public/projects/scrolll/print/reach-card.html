How a paid view works

Buy attention that pays people back.

Verified humans with stated interests. A disclosed share goes to the person watching. You pay only for posts that were actually on screen.

One paid view, start to finish

1
They pick the topic

People opt into ad topics and set a “never” list. You only reach people who said yes to yours.

2
Your post runs, labeled

Always marked “Sponsored” in the feed. Each person caps how many they see per hour.

3
Counted only if seen

Half the post on screen for 1 second (video: 2). The industry viewable bar. Scroll-pasts are free.

4
Your price is split

Viewer, the creator hosting the feed, and Scrolll. Split disclosed to people before they earn.

You don’t get
