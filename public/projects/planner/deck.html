The problem

Planners punish the week you missed, so people quit by February.

Two blank weeks. Starting again means facing them.

Sample person

Jess, 31

Buys a pretty planner every January. Misses one week. By February it's in a drawer, and she buys another next year.

Pattern: Strava found the biggest drop in January workouts in the second week (17.3% fewer uploads in an analysis of 31.5M activities). BikeRadar and Coach, reporting Strava data. Jess is a sample, not a real customer.

How it works

Pick, print, cut, play. The spinner works with a pencil and a paperclip.

Pick

Take the quiz, or choose by how you're stuck.

Print

Free PDF at home, or the $39 printed kit.

Cut

Spinners, dice, sliders, dials. Dashed lines show where.

Play

Flick the clip. Where it stops is your task.

The first 1,000 people

Planner people already trade printables. Start there.

  1. Five talks, five intros. End each with "Who do you know who buys a planner every January?"
  2. Alexandra posts her own quiz result in r/planners, r/bulletjournal and #stationerycore.
  3. The quiz link travels. A friend opens "Sam kept 6 pages. Which would you keep?" and plays the same ten.
  4. One 7-day ad test on Pinterest and Instagram, US women 22 to 40 into planners and journaling.

Post on the days people restart:

New Year, back to school, Sunday night, the week after a missed week.

Plan, not results. Channels from the share and validation notes (22 Nov 2026). #stationerycore: 57M TikTok posts (Modern Retail, 9 Dec 2025).
