Problem

Planners punish the week you missed.

Dated pages turn one bad week into a stack of blanks. Most people stop there, and the goal goes with the planner.

The moment

Three empty weeks in March. Starting again means facing them, or buying a new planner. Illustrative, not a real user

What we know

Undated planners are already a paid category: Passion Planner, BestSelf and Erin Condren all sell them.

Solution

One goal on paper, and a way back after any gap.
