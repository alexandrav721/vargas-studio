Make the spinner — no scissors needed

  1. Write eight tasks in the list. Keep them all doable today.
  2. Hold a pencil tip upright on the centre dot, through one end of a paperclip.
  3. Flick the paperclip. Where it stops is your task.
  4. Start it within two minutes. Cross it off, spin again later.

Want a fancy one? Cut out the wheel and the arrow on page 6 and join them with a brad.

Energy dial

Turn it each morning. Then pick from the menu.
Base wheel
Top cover

Make it

  1. Glue this page to cardstock. Cut out both circles.
  2. Cut out the white window in the top cover.
  3. Stack the cover on the base. Push a brad through both centres and open its legs.
  4. Each morning, turn the window to how you feel. Then open the energy menu on the next page.
  5. Tape the base to the inside of the cover so it lives with your planner.

✂ Make it pull-tab
See it move

Progress pull-tab

Pull the strip up as your goal moves.

Make it

  1. Glue the page to cardstock if you can.
  2. Cut out the window in the meter, and cut the two short slits above and below it.
  3. Cut out the strip along its dashed edge.
  4. Thread the strip in through the bottom slit, behind the window, and out the top slit.
  5. Each time you reach a milestone, pull the strip so the colour rises to that mark.

Make the scratch squares

  1. Write a small reward in each dashed square (a coffee out, an episode, a nap). Ask a friend to fill them so it's a surprise.
  2. Cover each square with clear packing tape.
  3. Mix 2 parts acrylic paint to 1 part dish soap. Paint two coats over the tape. Let it dry.
  4. Finish the task, then scratch with a coin. Shortcut: use ready-made scratch-off stickers.

✂ Make it envelope
Unload

Worry envelope

Write the worry. Fold it away. Open it on a better day.

Make it

  1. Cut out the diamond along the dashed line.
  2. Fold the left and right points in along the dotted square.
  3. Fold the bottom point up and glue its edges.
  4. Write worries on slips of paper, tuck them in, fold the top down. Open it next week — most will have shrunk.
