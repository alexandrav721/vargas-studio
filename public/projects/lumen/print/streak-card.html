Stuck games

Cut out. Keep one in your wallet. Name the fog, play one, check in.

Cut on the dashed lines
Stuck game 1

Inherited voice

Whose line is it?

  1. Write the sentence stopping you.
  2. Whose words were they first?
  3. Rewrite it in your own voice.
  4. Do one 2-minute move.

Stuck game 2

Loyalty guilt

Both, not either

  1. Name who you fear letting down.
  2. Write how your goal helps them too.
  3. Give them 10 minutes. Then 10 to you.

Stuck game 3

Attention stolen

Other room, 10 minutes

  1. Phone goes in another room.
  2. Set a 10-minute timer.
  3. Count 3, 2, 1. Start the first line.

Stuck game 4

No clear next step

Film-able move

  1. Write the next step.
  2. Could someone film you doing it? If not, shrink it.
  3. Shrink until it takes 2 minutes. Do it.
