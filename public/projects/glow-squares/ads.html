What every frame follows

  • The product line is always the same eight words: "Dark chocolate. 2.5 g collagen. One square after dinner." In a Reel it is on screen by 2.5 to 3 s, top third.
  • No studies, no skin, no results, no faces next to the word Glow. The study wording and the honesty line live on the website.
  • Any price on screen reads "$49/month, renews monthly, skip or cancel anytime". Gift prices say they don't renew.
  • The real snap opens every Reel once it's recorded. If the samples don't snap, the sound changes before any ad runs.
  • Creator versions carry "Paid partnership" on every post.

1 Collagen per serving as printed on two beauty-gummy labels (100 mg and 200 mg per 2 gummies), listed in the competitor research, Nov 2026. Names stay off the ad until the attorney clears the comparison.
2 Our planned dose per square, 2.5 g Verisol collagen peptides. To be confirmed on the final Nutrition Facts panel.
