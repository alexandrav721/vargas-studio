Johanna J
  1. 1

    Her story

    Hi, I'm Johanna. I bought the collagen powder, like everyone. Then it sat in the cupboard while I ate candy after dinner, every night. So I stopped trying to become a scoop person and made the thing I'd actually eat: one dark chocolate square with 2.5 g of collagen in it, and a note underneath. Johanna J

  2. 2

    The tray

    Thirty squares, numbered like rooms. One a night, after dinner. Room 01 is top left, for anyone who needs to know where to start. The gold one is not a mistake.

  3. 3

    Night 23

    Night 23 of 30. There's a note under every wrapper, and you only get tonight's. I won't tell you what Night 24 says. It involves the laundry.

  4. 4

    The hanger

    Every box comes with one of these. Hang it on any door that closes. What happens on the other side is your business.

  5. 5

    The snap

    The samples arrive in December. The first thing I'm filming is the snap: quiet kitchen, phone very close, ten takes. You'll get the good one.

  6. 6

    One line

    The whole thing fits on one line, so here it is on one line. It's sold as chocolate, with Nutrition Facts on the box. No scoop, no shaker, no pill.

  7. 7

    Night 3

    Night 3. The laundry comes back later in the month. It does not take being ignored well.

  8. 8

    The math

    $49 a month gets you 30 squares, which is $1.63 a night. It renews monthly, and you can skip or cancel anytime. One box on its own is $59. People on the waitlist get their first box for $39, until the first 500 boxes are gone.

  9. 9

    Pre-orders

    Pre-orders open January 11. Boxes go out in early February, before the 14th. The waitlist hears first. Link in bio.
