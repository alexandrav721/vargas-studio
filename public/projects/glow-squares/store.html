Why I put collagen in candy.

I'm 34. I wanted to take better care of myself, so I bought the collagen powder. And then I didn't take it. Every morning it was one more scoop, one more thing to remember, one more taste I didn't like.

What I never forgot was candy. Every night, something sweet after dinner.

So I stopped trying to be a different person and made the thing I'd actually eat: one chocolate square with 2.5 g of collagen in it, and a note underneath to make me laugh.

Turns out a lot of women were stuck in the same spot.

Johanna J, founder
