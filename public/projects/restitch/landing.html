Questions

Before you open it.

Is this an AI pattern maker?

No. The page averages the pixels of your photo in your browser. No model invents a measurement, and there is no pattern.

Where does my photo go?

It is drawn on a canvas on the page. Saved flips live in this browser's storage. There is no upload server and no account.

I don't own a sewing machine.

Pick Beginner. Every read includes a no-sew flip and a hand-stitch flip.

Inches or centimeters?

Either. Type in your unit. The page repeats what you typed and never converts it.
